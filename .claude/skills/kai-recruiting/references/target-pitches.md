# Target Company Pitches

Use these closing lines when writing company-specific cover letters or "why us" answers.
Always combine with the standard opening from the About section.

---

## Anthropic

**Closing line:**
> I want to bring practitioner credibility to how enterprise customers actually land Claude in production — I've lived the gap between what AI demos promise and what enterprise delivery demands, and I want to help close it from the inside.

**Angle:** Kai has shipped a production AI system using Claude/Anthropic API in his own agentic workflows. He understands the enterprise buyer's pain firsthand. He can be a credible voice to customers who are trying to do what he's already done.

---

## Google Cloud

**Closing line:**
> I want to help enterprise teams realize the value of the CX Agent Studio, Dialogflow CX, and Vertex AI platform I've built on firsthand — as someone who has taken a Fortune 500 client from conversation design to production on Google's stack, I know exactly where customers get stuck.

**Angle:** Firsthand DFCX + Google Enterprise CX Agent Studio + GCP production experience is rare. Most Google Cloud sellers haven't built on it themselves. Kai has.

## Salesforce AgentForce
