## Style Rules

- Lead every summary with the **production signal** — "ships to production, not demos"
- **"Fortune 500 client"** always — never name the client, never say "major US utility"
- Dialogflow CX and Google Enterprise CX Agent Studio are **parallel** — list them as "Dialogflow CX and Google Enterprise CX Agent Studio" not "Google Enterprise CX Agent Studio (Dialogflow CX)"
- MS is always **"MS Design"** — never the full degree name
- Never mention Genesys
- Do not claim Kai built/architected webhook services — he led troubleshooting and resolution
- Keep bullets **outcome-led** — what shipped, what was owned, what was tested (772 is a real number, use it)
- The **"strategy in the morning, session logs in the afternoon"** line is approved and effective — use it
- For target company pitches, **swap the closing line** per company (see references/target-pitches.md)
- Keep tone: confident, specific, practitioner — never hype, never vague
